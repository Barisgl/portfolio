# Technische Hochschule Ingolstadt (THI)

## Digital Procurement and Sustainable Supply Chain Management (M.Sc.) – Comprehensive Profile

This document outlines the general structure, ideal candidate profile, core competencies to be acquired, and post-graduation career opportunities for the **Digital Procurement and Sustainable Supply Chain Management (M.Sc.)** (*Digitaler Einkauf und Nachhaltiges Supply Chain Management*) master's program at Technische Hochschule Ingolstadt.

### 1. General Program Overview

* **Degree and Duration:** Master of Science (M.Sc.) – 3 Semesters (90 ECTS)

* **Language of Instruction:** Primarily German (approx. 30% of the course content is taught in English; opportunities for a double degree or additional certificate in cooperation with California State University are available).

* **Study Model:** Can be pursued full-time (*Vollzeit*) or as a dual-study program (*Dual*) integrated with a partner company.

* **Core Focus:** Combining traditional procurement processes with digital tools (data science, artificial intelligence, blockchain), cost engineering, and sustainable/resilient supply chains.

### 2. Ideal Student Profile (Who Is It For?)

This program is designed for candidates who want to bridge the gap between the technical world and commercial/strategic decision-making processes:

* **Academic Background:** Graduates from STEM (MINT) fields such as industrial, mechanical, or electrical engineering who want to specialize in commercial-strategic areas, **or** business/economics graduates with a strong interest in technical processes and data analysis.

* **Analytical and Data-Driven Mindset:** Individuals who prefer making decisions based on data analysis, Python, cost models, and market metrics rather than intuition alone.

* **Sustainability Awareness:** Candidates who view carbon footprint reduction, ethical sourcing, regulatory compliance, and supply chain resilience as the business models of the future.

* **Communication and Negotiation Skills:** People capable of engaging in intercultural communication with international suppliers and taking an active role in contract management and negotiation processes.

### 3. Core Competencies Acquired During the Program

The technical and managerial skill set graduates will possess based on the curriculum modules:

| Competency Area | Key Courses / Content | Practical Skills Acquired | 
| :--- | :--- | :--- | 
| **Digital Procurement & Data Science** | *Digital Procurement & Data Science* | Automating procurement processes using data analysis with Python, blockchain, and digital twins. | 
| **Cost & Risk Engineering** | *Cost Engineering & Riskmanagement* | Analyzing cost drivers during product development and manufacturing phases; performing *Target Costing* and *Should Costing* calculations. | 
| **Sustainable Supply Chain** | *Nachhaltiges Supply Chain Management* | Establishing global, crisis-resilient supply networks and managing sustainability KPIs. | 
| **Strategic Sourcing & Negotiation** | *Strategisches Beschaffungsmanagement, Verhandlungstechnik* | Conducting *Make-or-Buy* analyses, digital contract management, and advanced negotiation techniques. | 
| **Innovation & Technology** | *Technology Development & Innovation Management* | Technology foresight, intellectual property (IP) management, and supplier-driven innovation processes. | 

### 4. Post-Graduation Career and Position Profile

Because Ingolstadt is a major automotive and technology hub (home to Audi and its extensive supplier ecosystem), graduates of this program are in direct demand, particularly among industrial and manufacturing giants:

* **Strategic / Technical Buyer:** Sourcing complex engineering components or software from global suppliers while balancing cost and performance.

* **Supply Chain & Risk Manager:** Forecasting global supply disruptions and establishing alternative supply routes and sustainability standards.

* **Cost & Value Engineer:** Optimizing production and procurement costs while a product is still in the design phase.

* **Digital Procurement Consultant / Specialist:** Transforming corporate procurement departments using ERP systems, data analytics, and AI tools.

* **Supplier Quality & Development Manager:** Auditing and developing the technical process capabilities of suppliers.
